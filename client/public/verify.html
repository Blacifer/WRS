Verify a release certificate or a wagon passport

Checked here, in this browser, with no request to any server. Paste the certificate's JSON, or the passport file's text — or open the file.

What this proves, and what it does not

A pass means: the bytes shown were signed by whoever holds the private half of the key whose fingerprint is shown, and nothing in them has changed since. A fail names what is wrong.

It does not prove the key belongs to WRS Raipur. The certificate prints the shop's key fingerprint; the shop publishes the key at /api/audit/certificate-key. If the fingerprint here matches the one you got from the shop by another route, the document is theirs.
