WRS RAIPUR — CBC AND DRAFT GEAR SECTION — WALL CHARTS
Transcribed from photographs of the boards above the reconditioning benches, September 2026.
These are the shop's own gauges and limits as posted on the floor. Where a gauge number was
not fully legible in the photograph it is marked (confirm on the board). Nothing here is from
the manual; it is what the section works to. Source label in this index: CBC_WALL.

BOARD 1 — CBC COMPONENT DROPPING FROM WAGON (FLOW CHART)

Start: CBC component dropping from wagon.

Knuckle and lock: removing of knuckle and lock from wagon; cleaning of knuckle and lock with
the help of wire brush; inspection for manufacturer name, grade of material (HTEA/UHTE),
casting no., year built — if manufacturer name and year built and material grade is not
found: CONDEMNED; inspection for crack with the help of magnifying glass and wear with the
help of gauges — ok; marking on knuckle (workshop code, dispatch no., POH date); fitted in
wagon.

Coupler body: removing of coupler body from wagon; cleaning of coupler body with the help of
wire brush / grinding machine fitted with wire brush; inspection for manufacturer name and
grade of material (HTEA/UHTE), casting no., year built — if not found: CONDEMNED; inspection
for crack with the help of magnifying glass and wear with the help of gauges — ok; marking on
coupler body (workshop code, dispatch no., POH date); fitted in wagon.

Draft gear and yoke: removing of draft gear and yoke assembly from wagon; removing of draft
gear and front follower from yoke; external cleaning of draft gear with the help of wire
brush; inspection of manufacturer name, year built and inspection of housing and its
components, gauging of housing — centre wedge, 30 degree shoe or rear wall crack for RF-361
draft gear; if housing wear or crack: CONDEMNED; otherwise draft gear reconditioning;
preshortening and gauging; marking on draft gear (workshop code, dispatch no., POH date);
fitted in wagon.

Yoke: removing of yoke from draft gear; cleaning of yoke with the help of wire brush;
inspection of manufacturer name, year built and gauging — if manufacturer name, year built is
not found: CONDEMNED; marking on yoke (workshop code, dispatch no., POH date); fitted in wagon.

Models on the bench: model of CBC (coupler, striker casting, front follower, yoke, draft
gear, back stopper, yoke pin support, yoke support plate, anchor support plate); cross section
model of coupler; cross section model of knuckle; lock, lock lift and knuckle thrower.
Draft gear cut-away models labelled MK-50 DG, RF-361, MK70E, MK325, POWER GUARD, F-325 G
TWIN PACK.

BOARD 7 — MARK-50 PLATE, WEDGE, SHOE AND SEAT GAUGES

OUTER STATIONARY PLATE GAUGE (BE/91-61-3)
Gap 0.38 mm or more — plate is scrapped. Size: 24.64 mm.

WEDGE SHOE GAUGE (BE/91-61-2)
Gap 0.38 mm or more — shoe is scrapped. Contact with the surface — shoe is scrapped.

CENTRE WEDGE GAUGE (BE/91-61-1)
Gap 0.38 mm or more — wedge is scrapped. Gauge should not contact; if contact — wedge is
scrapped.

SPRING SEAT GAUGE (BE/91-61-5)
Gauge to contact here; if non contact — scrap spring seat. If 0.38 mm or more gap — scrap
spring seat.

TAPER STATIONARY PLATE GAUGE (BE/91-61-4)
Gauge should not pass this line. NO GO gauge: 0.25 mm.

MOVABLE PLATE GAUGE (BE/91-61-10)
Place gauge with base at centre and rotate 180 degrees; if no contact of base with movable
plate, it is scrap. NO GO gauge: 24.13 mm. NO GO gauge: 211.17 mm.

BOARD 8 — SPRING SORTING BENCH (CASNUB), AS SEEN

Snubber spring gauge SSG-02: a post with a collar; the spring is dropped over the post and its
free height read against the marks. Label (ASKIB, at lab / on site): descr. SNUBBER SPRING
(HS); ID SSG-02; issued to SSE/CWM RWSS Raipur SECR; cert no. 1251122-04-125; calibrated on:
blank; calibration valid upto: blank.

Sorted springs carry a painted ring on the coil in the band colour (green, yellow seen on the
bogie), so the band a spring was sorted into is visible on the wagon after assembly.

Wheel sets carry chalked measurements on the wheel disc (tread diameter and date) and the
slack adjuster carries a painted identification number (K-1894 seen).
